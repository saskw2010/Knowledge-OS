# KOS Decision Record (KDR)

Decision ID:
KDR-0002

Title:
Preserve multiple classification systems through crosswalks

Date:
2026-08-06

Authors:
Project Team

Status:
Accepted

Priority:
Critical

Category:
Architecture

---

## Context

Human knowledge has been organized differently by philosophers, civilizations, universities, research institutions, libraries, bibliometric platforms, professions, and language models. These systems answer different questions and cannot be safely collapsed into one hierarchy.

---

## Decision

Knowledge-OS will ingest and version each classification system independently. Original nodes, identifiers, labels, hierarchy, purpose, source, and release information will be preserved. Crosswalks will connect source-specific `ClassificationNode` entities to `CanonicalConcept` entities and, where justified, to nodes in other systems.

The graph is the underlying representation; each tree or hierarchy is a source-specific view.

---

## Rationale

This architecture preserves disagreement and provenance while supporting comparison, navigation, semantic normalization, and multi-perspective reasoning.

---

## Assumptions

- No single classification is sufficient for all project purposes.
- Crosswalks may be exact, close, broad, narrow, partial, or disputed.
- Source systems may change over time and require versioned imports.

---

## Alternatives Considered

### Select one master classification

**Pros:** simpler hierarchy and user interface.

**Cons:** introduces institutional bias and loses alternative perspectives.

### Build a new universal tree first

**Pros:** full control over structure.

**Cons:** premature, difficult to validate, and likely to reproduce hidden biases.

### Flatten all systems into tags

**Pros:** easy ingestion.

**Cons:** loses hierarchy, purpose, historical context, and relation semantics.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

- Preserves original systems and their intent.
- Enables historical and institutional comparisons.
- Supports reversible normalization.

### Negative

- More entities, mappings, and validation states.
- Crosswalk maintenance becomes ongoing work.

### Unknown

- Some systems may have licensing or redistribution constraints that limit ingestion depth.

---

## Risks

Users may mistake one source view for the canonical truth, or model-generated mappings for verified equivalence.

---

## Mitigation

Expose system name, version, mapping type, provenance, status, and confidence in all APIs and interfaces.
