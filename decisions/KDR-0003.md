# KOS Decision Record (KDR)

Decision ID:
KDR-0003

Title:
Teacher-to-student model training pipeline

Date:
2026-08-06

Authors:
Project Team

Status:
Proposed

Priority:
Medium

Category:
AI Training

---

## Context

Knowledge-OS is expected eventually to generate validated datasets that can support teacher-model review, distillation, and smaller student models. The repository does not yet have enough validated graph coverage, evaluation design, or dataset governance to accept a production training architecture.

---

## Decision

Treat the Teacher → Dataset Review → Student Model pipeline as a proposed future capability, not as part of the v0.1 MVP. No training artifact may be described as production-ready until its source knowledge, licensing, provenance, contamination controls, evaluation split, and independent validation are defined.

---

## Rationale

Deferring implementation avoids building a training stack on unstable schemas and weakly validated knowledge. It also prevents circular evaluation where the same model generates, validates, and judges its own outputs.

---

## Assumptions

- A validated Knowledge Graph and Dataset Factory will precede model training.
- Independent judges, human review, or external benchmarks will be required.
- Training rights and content licenses must be tracked at sample level.

---

## Alternatives Considered

### Train a student model immediately

**Pros:** early demonstration.

**Cons:** unreliable data, unclear legal status, and no defensible evaluation.

### Use one teacher model for generation and judging

**Pros:** simple and inexpensive.

**Cons:** circular evaluation and shared failure modes.

### Exclude model training permanently

**Pros:** narrower and safer product scope.

**Cons:** removes an important downstream use case.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

- Keeps v0.1 focused on classification ingestion, crosswalks, provenance, and validation.
- Forces evaluation and licensing to be designed before training.

### Negative

- Delays visible model-training results.

### Unknown

- Final teacher, student, and judge architectures will depend on future model availability and cost.

---

## Risks

Future implementation may still inherit model bias, evaluation leakage, and dataset contamination.

---

## Mitigation

Require independent evaluation, frozen test sets, provenance-preserving samples, and explicit release gates.
