# Knowledge-OS Mission

**Status:** Proposed foundation addition.

## Mission statement

Organize human knowledge for trustworthy AI, research, and education through a provenance-aware, multi-perspective Meta-Knowledge Graph that preserves the structure and purpose of each source classification system.

## Operational mission

Knowledge-OS will:

1. Register historical, institutional, academic, library, bibliometric, professional, and model-generated classification systems without flattening them into one hierarchy.
2. Preserve every source system's original nodes, paths, versions, purposes, licenses, and provenance.
3. Normalize equivalent concepts into canonical concept nodes while retaining alternative labels and disputed mappings.
4. Represent knowledge as a graph with explicit, typed, directional, confidence-scored relations.
5. Expand and validate nodes through repeatable prompts, authoritative sources, multiple models, and human review.
6. Generate traceable educational, evaluation, and training datasets only from validated graph content.
7. Support teacher-model review and student-model distillation without presenting generated structures as unquestioned truth.

## Success conditions

The mission is being achieved when:

- a concept can be traced back to every system and source that classifies it;
- disagreements remain visible and queryable;
- generated nodes are distinguishable from source-extracted nodes;
- dataset items can be traced to graph claims and evidence;
- validation and licensing status can block unsafe publication or training;
- adding a new classification system does not require rewriting existing systems.

## Out of scope for the foundation phase

- Declaring one universal and final tree of knowledge.
- Treating an LLM-generated classification as an authoritative standard.
- Training on material without provenance and legal-use records.
- Deep recursive expansion before stop conditions and quality gates are operational.
