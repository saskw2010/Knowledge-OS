# Data and Legal Policy

## Principle

Knowledge-OS must be built from data that can be lawfully collected, stored, transformed, redistributed, and used for the intended research or training purpose.

## Required metadata for every source

- Source identity and owner.
- Retrieval date and exact locator.
- License or contractual permission.
- Allowed uses and restrictions.
- Attribution requirements.
- Redistribution and derivative-work rights.
- Personal, confidential, culturally sensitive, or restricted content indicators.
- Retention and deletion obligations.

## Source classes

### Preferred

- Public-domain material.
- Openly licensed datasets and publications.
- Official classifications released for reuse.
- Data supplied through explicit agreements.
- Internally authored and reviewed content.

### Restricted pending review

- Copyrighted books and articles without clear reuse rights.
- Licensed databases whose terms limit extraction or model training.
- Library scans and digital collections with item-specific rights.
- Personal or confidential information.
- Traditional or indigenous knowledge requiring community governance.

### Prohibited

- Unlawfully obtained data.
- Data collected by bypassing access controls.
- Content used contrary to a binding license or agreement.
- Personal data without a valid basis, safeguards, and documented purpose.

## Model-generated knowledge

Model output is not treated as a clean source merely because it was generated. Store model name, version, prompt, parameters, date, raw response, and validation state. Generated claims require independent verification before approval or dataset publication.

## Library and institutional partnerships

A partnership with a library, university, ministry, or archive must define permitted collections, digitization rights, OCR and transformation rights, model-training rights, attribution, access control, retention, publication, and withdrawal procedures in writing.

## Dataset release gate

No dataset artifact may be published or used for training until provenance coverage, license compatibility, privacy review, duplication checks, and human approval meet the project policy.
