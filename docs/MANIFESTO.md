# Knowledge-OS Manifesto

**Status:** Proposed foundation addition.

Knowledge should be open where law and rights permit, traceable to its sources, explicit about uncertainty, plural in perspective, and extensible without erasing history.

We therefore commit to the following:

1. **Knowledge is not one tree.** Trees are useful views; the underlying representation is a graph.
2. **Perspective is data.** A classification is inseparable from its author, institution, purpose, period, version, and method.
3. **Provenance before confidence.** A confident claim without traceable support is not production-ready knowledge.
4. **Extraction and inference are different.** Source statements, mappings, model proposals, and human decisions must carry distinct statuses.
5. **Disagreement is preserved.** Competing classifications and disputed boundaries remain queryable rather than being silently averaged away.
6. **Canonical does not mean absolute.** A canonical concept is an integration anchor, not a declaration that all traditions use one meaning.
7. **Validation is continuous.** Graph publication, dataset generation, and training require separate quality gates.
8. **Lawful data use is architecture.** Licensing, permissions, privacy, and attribution are system requirements, not administrative afterthoughts.
9. **Multilingual knowledge is first-class.** English labels cannot be treated as the only canonical conceptual structure.
10. **Humans remain accountable.** Models may propose structures, but approval, governance, and high-impact release decisions require responsible human review.
11. **Knowledge products must be reversible.** Every dataset item and generated explanation should be traceable back to graph claims and sources.
12. **Growth must not destroy meaning.** Recursive expansion stops when evidence, distinction, usefulness, or quality no longer justifies additional nodes.
