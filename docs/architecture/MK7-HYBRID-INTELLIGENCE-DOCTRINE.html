MK7 Hybrid Intelligence Doctrine

Sky365 Architecture Principle

Enterprise-first intelligence: controlled at the top, adaptive at the bottom.
ذكاء مؤسسي أولًا: تحكم في الأعلى، وتكيّف في الأسفل.

Core doctrine

MK7 combines explicit controllable business adapters with latent learned specialization. Both live together; business routing stays above latent expert routing.

Architecture

MK7 Semantic Router
  ↓
Business / Domain Selection
  ↓
Domain MoLoRA Router
  ↓
Latent LoRA Experts
  ↓
Gemma Base Model

Key insight

Kimi/GLM-style native MoE routes latent experts inside the model. MK7 adds an explicit enterprise control plane above learned specialization for auditability, tenant policy, and governance.

Doctrine

Route aggressively at every layer. Use the smallest capable model first. Keep explicit business control above latent specialization.

Document once as doctrine, distribute many times as implementation guidance.
